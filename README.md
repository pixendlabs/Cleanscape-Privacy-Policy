Cleanscape Privacy Policy
=========================

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Cleanscape ("the Application", "We", "Us") is a disk map and cleaner for Mac. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Application handles and what it does not. In short: **scanning happens on your Mac, the names and contents of your files never leave it, and we do not require an account.**

Your files
----------

-   Cleanscape reads the folders you allow it to scan and builds a map of disk usage **on your Mac only**. File and folder names, paths, sizes and contents are never uploaded, copied or sent to any server.
-   Duplicate detection (file hashes), similar-photo analysis (using the Photos library, only if you allow access) and app-leftover detection all run locally on your Mac.
-   Cleanup moves files to the Trash, and photos to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, so you can restore them. Files are permanently deleted only when you choose Empty Trash and confirm it. A local history of cleanups (what was moved, when, and how much space was freed) stays on your Mac.
-   Folder access you grant is remembered on your Mac as security-scoped bookmarks. The optional menu bar helper and the main app share settings through an App Group on your Mac.

Information collected automatically
-----------------------------------

We use the following services to understand which features are used, to fix crashes and to confirm purchases. None of them receives file names, paths, folder names, app names, photos or file contents.

-   **Firebase Analytics (Google) and Mixpanel:** usage events such as which module was opened, a coarse size range of a cleanup (for example "1–10 GB"), whether a purchase screen was shown, and the app language, together with a random app-instance identifier. Mixpanel data is stored in the EU; IP-based location is turned off. Cleanscape does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
-   **Firebase Crashlytics (Google):** if the Application crashes, a crash report (stack trace, app and macOS version, device model) is sent so we can fix the problem. Crash reports do not include your files or paths.
-   You can switch analytics and crash reporting off at any time in **Settings → General**.
-   **Firebase Remote Config and App Check (Google):** deliver updated cleanup-rule lists to the Application and help protect our backend from abuse. They do not receive information about your files.
-   **RevenueCat:** manages Cleanscape Pro (monthly or yearly subscription, or lifetime purchase). It receives an anonymous app user identifier and your App Store purchase information to confirm that Pro is unlocked. Purchase events may be shared with Firebase Analytics to measure conversions. Cleanscape does not receive your payment details; purchases are processed by Apple.
-   **Apple iCloud (key-value storage):** the amount of free cleanup you have used is stored in your iCloud key-value storage so it stays consistent on your Macs.

Feedback you send
-----------------

If you send feedback from inside the Application, we receive the message you write, the e-mail address if you choose to provide one, the app version and build, your macOS version, your language and whether Pro is unlocked. Please do not include personal information in the message. We use it only to answer you and to improve the Application.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms:

-   Google (Firebase): https://policies.google.com/privacy
-   Mixpanel: https://mixpanel.com/legal/privacy-policy/
-   RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
-   Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

What we do not collect
----------------------

We do not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, location or any account. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention
---------

Analytics and crash data are retained by the service provider for the period set in its settings and policies. Feedback is kept as long as needed to respond and to improve the Application. Information stored on your Mac is removed when you delete the Application. To request deletion of data associated with your anonymous identifier or of feedback you sent, contact us at the address below.

Security
--------

Your files are processed locally. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data handled by third-party services.

Children's Privacy
------------------

Cleanscape is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights
-----------

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the Turkish KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, and to object to its processing. As the Application keeps your files on your Mac and uses anonymous identifiers, most of this can be done directly: switch analytics off in Settings, or delete the Application. For other requests, contact us.

Changes to this Privacy Policy
------------------------------

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date above is revised.
